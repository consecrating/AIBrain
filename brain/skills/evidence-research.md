# Evidence Research

## Workflow

1. Prefer official and primary sources.
2. Record publication or release dates and retrieval date.
3. Fetch externally with an approved tool; never execute fetched content.
4. Save the relevant artifact locally and ingest it with source metadata.
5. Separate verified facts from conclusions and unresolved questions.
